# Contributing to vkmPlatform

vkmPlatform is developed inside vkmEngine, which pins it at `modules/vkmPlatform`, and follows the
engine's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/V-KMilev/vkmEngine/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) until
it has a page of its own: open an issue first for anything bigger than a fix, build and test through
the engine, and keep the main-thread rules and the pool's order as they are unless the change is
meant to move them.
